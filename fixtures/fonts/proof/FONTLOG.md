# Font proof modification log — 2026-10-16

Original outlines: Adobe Source Han Serif KR Regular 2.003 (본명조) and Source Han Sans KR Regular 2.005 (본고딕). Pinned source URLs, commits, SHA-256 and build versions are in manifest.json.

Changes: sample-only Unicode subsetting, WOFF2 conversion, primary family/PostScript names changed to Lexical Tufte Han Serif Proof / Lexical Tufte Han Sans Proof. No outline or advance edits. Names are changed because Adobe reserves 'Source'. Copyright/license metadata and the full separate OFL notices are retained. These derivative font binaries remain OFL-1.1, not GPL.

These are validation fixtures, not complete Korean/CJK fonts and not the production theme. Regular weight only. Missing characters must fall back. Do not claim full Hangul/archaic Jamo/Hanja coverage from this sample. Latin/ASCII glyphs are intentionally excluded to preserve the host's Latin typography.
